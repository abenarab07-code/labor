## 1. Brand Core

### The idea

Dr Tarfaya is not positioned as a generic sample collection point. It is the meeting point between routine medical analysis and specialist hematology interpretation. The brand turns an anxious, technical subject into a clear next step.

### Mission

We make blood analysis and specialist hematology easier to understand and access, so every patient can move forward with clarity.

### Vision

A laboratory experience where scientific precision never loses the human being behind the sample.

### Positioning

For patients, families and referring physicians in El Bouni and Annaba who need reliable blood-related investigation, Dr Tarfaya is a medical analysis laboratory with specialist hematology consultation. Unlike a generic lab message focused only on test lists, the brand connects the sample, expert interpretation and the next clinical step.

### Promise

**Comprendre le sang. Éclairer le diagnostic.**

### Signature line

**Votre sang raconte. Nous savons le lire.**

Use the signature on the home-page hero and major campaigns. Use the promise in institutional or physician-facing contexts.

### Reason to believe

- Laboratory analyses and blood sampling in one clear patient journey.
- Specialist hematology consultation for cases requiring deeper interpretation.
- Specific expertise highlighted by the client: blood smear and bone-marrow cytology.
- Direct phone and WhatsApp access for appointment requests and practical questions.

Do not claim accreditation, turnaround times, emergency availability or guaranteed diagnosis unless the clinic supplies verified proof.

## 3. Voice & Tone

### We are

| Trait   | We do                                   | We do not                            |
| ------- | --------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Precise | Name the service and the next step      | Drown patients in unexplained jargon |
| Human   | Address the person, not only the sample | Use fear to force a booking          |
| Calm    | Use short, grounded sentences           | Promise certainty before examination |
| Expert  | Explain the role of hematology clearly  | Sound arrogant or inaccessible       |

### Language system

- French is the primary website language.
- A short Arabic clarification may accompany the hero: `تحاليل طبية وطب أمراض الدم`.
- Use familiar Algerian terms where useful: `ordonnance`, `bilan`, `prélèvement`, `résultats`.
- Prefer `demander un rendez-vous` to `réserver instantanément`; confirmation depends on availability.

### Approved examples

- `Une analyse ne se résume pas à un chiffre.`
- `Le bon examen. Au bon moment. Avec la bonne lecture.`
- `Avant de venir, écrivez-nous sur WhatsApp avec le nom de l'analyse.`
- `Une urgence vitale nécessite les services d'urgence; le formulaire du site n'est pas un service d'urgence.`

### Avoid

- `Le meilleur laboratoire`
- `Résultats garantis`
- `Diagnostic immédiat`
- `Disponible 24/7`
- Any unverified count, rating, certification or turnaround time

## 4. Visual Identity

### Memorable device: The Diagnostic Field

The orbit from the existing logo becomes a flexible circular field: lens, scan, cell, pathway and focus. It may crop beyond the canvas, hold photography or trace a subtle animated line. This is the one visual device that should remain recognizable across the website, social media and print.

### Logo system

- **Primary lockup:** diagnostic-field symbol + `DR TARFAYA` + descriptor.
- **Compact mark:** symbol only for favicon, avatar and small UI.
- **Monochrome:** Midnight Ink on light backgrounds; Plasma Ivory on dark backgrounds.
- Keep clear space equal to the inner cell diameter around the mark.
- Minimum digital width: 148 px for the full lockup; 24 px for the mark.
- Never reuse the original raster screenshot as a production logo. It is a concept reference only.
- Do not stretch, bevel, shadow, rotate or place the mark inside another badge.

Oxygen Coral is a signal, not a background system. Keep it under roughly 10% of a composition.

### Typography

- **Instrument Serif:** large editorial statements and pull quotes. Use regular weight and expressive italics.
- **Manrope:** navigation, body, labels, forms and Arabic fallback.
- Headlines should be short, line-height 0.92-1.05 and use optical spacing.
- Body text minimum 16 px; labels minimum 12 px with generous tracking.
- Avoid Inter, Roboto, Arial and generic corporate geometric display treatments.

### Photography & generated imagery

- Use real Dr Tarfaya footage for identity and authority. Never synthesize or alter her face.
- Pair real footage with generated scientific worlds: optical glass, blood cells, microscope fields and calm lab detail.
- Light is controlled, directional and credible; no glossy sci-fi holograms.
- Use asymmetrical framing and intentional negative space for copy.
- Do not show needles entering skin, distressed patients, gore or fake readable medical labels.

### Iconography

- 1.5 px outline at a 24 px base grid.
- Mostly circular geometry, square terminals and minimal corner rounding.
- The diagnostic-field orbit may surround one icon per major section, never every icon.

## 5. Interface System

- 8 px base spacing grid with larger editorial jumps of 48, 80 and 128 px.
- Corners are restrained: 6 px for controls, 18-28 px for major media, full circles only for the diagnostic field and compact actions.
- Buttons use compact rectangular capsules, not oversized pills.
- Motion: orbital draw, masked text reveal and quiet depth shift; all disabled for reduced motion.
- Break the grid with one overscaled circular diagnostic field per page.
- Maintain WCAG AA contrast for all body copy and interactive controls.
